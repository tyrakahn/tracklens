    TrackLens analyses metadata from Axis cameras to detect events such as people moving the wrong way, entering a forbidden zone, staying in one place for too long, following someone too closely (Tailgating) or lying on the ground (Man down). The events are shown to an operator in a web interface, together with a short video clip of what happened.

    The project is developed by a team of 28 students for Axis Communications, as part of the course TDDC88 Software Engineering at Linköping University. Since Sprint 4, CT1 handles deployment and the operator interface's statistics tab, and CT2–CT5 each own two events: both the rule-based and the statistical version, including the configuration settings in the interface. The teams work in a rolling flow: while the developers build one event, the analyst prepares the requirements for the next. A quality assurance team works across all teams.

    • CT1 – Deployment, the statistics tab and UI improvements; later quality measurements with the QA team
    • CT2 – Overcrowding and Dead zone
    • CT3 – Enter/exit vehicle and Car prowling
    • CT4 – Gathering and Sudden scatter
    • CT5 – Unusually high speed and Camera obstruction
    • QA – Quality assurance across all teams
    • Support – Project and process management
    1. November 2026 · Sprint 4

      Sprint 4 brings ten new events. CT2–CT5 each own two events, rule-based and statistical, including their settings in the operator interface: Overcrowding, Dead zone, Enter/exit vehicle, Car prowling, Gathering, Sudden scatter, Unusually high speed and Camera obstruction. CT1 focuses on deployment and the statistics tab, and a new quality assurance team works across all teams.

    2. 9 November 2026 · Sprint 3

      Sprint 3 started with a new team structure based on Axis's feedback: each team owns one event end to end. Two teams build the new events Tailgating and Man down, one team makes the statistical baseline learn over time and cleans up the code base, and the UI team adds the new events to the operator interface.

    3. 6 November 2026 · Milestone M3

      The Sprint 2 functionality was demonstrated live to Axis at their office. Rule-based detection is complete, including manual Long dwell, and statistics settings now take effect without a restart and can be switched off per camera. The Architecture Notebook 1.0, Test Plan 2.0, Test Report 1.0 and QA Plan 1.3 were released.

    4. October 2026

      Each camera can have its own rules for Wrong way, Forbidden zone and manual Long dwell, saved and changed independently. New detectors for Forbidden zone and manual Long dwell.

    5. 9 October 2026 · Sprint 2

      Sprint 2 started with a new team structure: four core teams (CT1–CT4) and a support team.
