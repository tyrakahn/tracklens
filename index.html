    TrackLens analyses metadata from Axis cameras to detect events such as people moving the wrong way, entering a forbidden zone or staying in one place for too long. The events are shown to an operator in a web interface, together with a short video clip of what happened.

    The project is developed by a team of 28 students in four core teams and a support team, for Axis Communications, as part of the course TDDC88 Software Engineering at Linköping University.

    1. November 2026

      Rule violations for Wrong way, Forbidden zone and manual Long dwell are now published as events. The Events page shows how a Long dwell event was flagged (statistical or manual) and how long the person stayed.

    2. October 2026

      Each camera can have its own rules for Wrong way, Forbidden zone and manual Long dwell, saved and changed independently. New detectors for Forbidden zone and manual Long dwell.
