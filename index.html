    TrackLens analyses metadata from Axis cameras to detect events such as people moving the wrong way, entering a forbidden zone, staying in one place for too long, following someone too closely (Tailgating) or lying on the ground (Man down). The events are shown to an operator in a web interface, together with a short video clip of what happened.

    The project is developed by a team of 28 students for Axis Communications, as part of the course TDDC88 Software Engineering at Linköping University. Since Sprint 3 the team is organised around events: each core team owns its event from detection to the operator interface.

    • CT1 – Data ingestion, event storage and the frame-by-frame metadata stream
    • CT2 – Tailgating
    • CT3 – Learning statistical baseline and code cleanup
    • CT4 – Man down
    • CT5 – Operator interface
    • Support – Project, product, process and R&D management
    1. 9 November 2026 · Sprint 3

      Sprint 3 started with a new team structure based on Axis's feedback: each team owns one event end to end. Two teams build the new events Tailgating and Man down, one team makes the statistical baseline learn over time and cleans up the code base, and the UI team adds the new events to the operator interface.

    2. 6 November 2026 · Milestone M3

      The Sprint 2 functionality was demonstrated live to Axis at their office. Rule-based detection is complete, including manual Long dwell, and statistics settings now take effect without a restart and can be switched off per camera. The Architecture Notebook 1.0, Test Plan 2.0, Test Report 1.0 and QA Plan 1.3 were released.

    3. October 2026

      Each camera can have its own rules for Wrong way, Forbidden zone and manual Long dwell, saved and changed independently. New detectors for Forbidden zone and manual Long dwell.

    4. 9 October 2026 · Sprint 2

      Sprint 2 started with a new team structure: four core teams (CT1–CT4) and a support team.
